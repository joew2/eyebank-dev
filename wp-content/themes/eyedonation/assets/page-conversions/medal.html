Gift of Life Donor Medal

The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration relies on selfless donors to help us provide donor eye tissue for sight-restoring cornea transplants, medical education and research into the treatment and cures of other blinding eye diseases. Those who have left such a priceless gift through eye donation, as well as their families for honoring their wishes, deserve our sincere gratitude.The Eye-Bank is pleased to offer donor families The New York State Gift of Life Donor Medal in recognition of their loved one’s generosity in helping to give the beautiful gift of sight.

Estate, Trust Gifts And The Paton Legacy Society

Make sight restoration your legacy.

Gifts made through estates and trusts allow you to maintain control of your assets while ensuring that your generosity continues to provide the gift of sight for years to come.

Since 1944, tens of thousands of people have had their sight restored because of the generosity of dedicated and thoughtful people who have made The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration part of their estate planning.

The Eye-Bank’s Paton Legacy Society was formed to honor founder, R. Townley Paton, M.D., by providing supporters an opportunity to contribute through their wills and trusts.

Monies generated by the Paton Legacy Society are invested in The Eye-Bank’s portfolio fund. This income is used to make sight restoration possible by increasing awareness about the need for eye donation and providing eye tissue for transplants and medical research.

For more information about adding The Eye-Bank to your estate or joining the Paton Legacy Society, please contact Robert Hankins, Major and Planned Gift Specialist, at rhankins@ebsr.org or (212) 742-9000 x111.

Social Media Fundraisers

OR

  2. On the left, click “Fundraisers” (Select “See more” if you don’t see “Fundraisers” listed)
  3. Under Raise Money, Click “Select Non-profit”
  4. Type into the search bar: Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration and select
  5. Enter your goal amount

  7. Email development@ebsr.org and notify staff of your fundraiser

  3. Crop and filter your post

The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration, a 501(c)(3) public charity, has helped restore sight to more than 70,000 individuals through cornea transplants since its founding in 1944. As an independent non-profit, it relies, in part, on contributions from individuals like you whose generosity enables The Eye-Bank to:

  • Give the gift of sight to more than 1,300 individuals every year regardless of age, race, ethnicity or gender.
  • Provide eye tissue to clinical researchers working to develop treatments for glaucoma, macular degeneration, and other eye diseases.
  • Increase eye and cornea donations through public and professional education and a multi-media marketing program.

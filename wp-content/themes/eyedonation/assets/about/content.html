Who We Are

The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration is the world’s first eye bank. Established in New York City in 1944, it is an independent, nonprofit organization dedicated to restoring sight through eye donation and corneal transplantation, as well as to providing ocular tissue for research into treatments and cures for other eye diseases.

Since The Eye-Bank was founded, the pioneering organization has helped provide the gift of sight to more than 76,000 individuals who would have otherwise been blind, and countless others have benefited from our contributions to ocular research.

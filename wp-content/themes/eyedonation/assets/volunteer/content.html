Volunteer

The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration relies on volunteers to help us educate the public about the importance of cornea donation. You can help make a difference by telling others about the need for eye donation and the good it can do. As part of our Speakers’ Bureau, donor family members, transplant recipients and others who are interested in supporting The Eye-Bank’s mission can join our outreach efforts at schools, health fairs, civic, legislative and community events.

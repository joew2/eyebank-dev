Employment

The Eye-Bank for Sight Restoration is able to provide donor eye tissue for ocular surgeries that give the gift of sight to more than 1,300 men, women and children each year thanks to our generous eye donors, skilled cornea surgeons and the collective efforts of The Eye-Bank staff who play an important role in helping return individuals to productive lives with sight.

Located in Manhattan and serving New York City, Long Island and the Lower Hudson Valley, The Eye-Bank employs professionals in a variety of departments including:

Communications

  • Traditional and social media
  • Newsletter and print materials
  • Public education activities

Development

  • Fundraising
  • Special events

Ocular Laboratory

  • Laboratory technicians
  • Transplant coordinators
  • Family services coordinators

Professional Education

  • Hospital and community outreach

Quality Assurance

  • Regulatory Compliance

For information about career opportunities and current job openings at The Eye-Bank, submit a resume and cover letter to HR@ebsr.org.

If you are a student interested in applying for an internship, submit a resume and cover letter HR@ebsr.org.

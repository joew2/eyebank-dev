Become a Donor

Donating your eyes and other organs and tissues at death can restore sight and health to persons in need. Eye donations are used for sight-saving cornea transplants, reconstructive eye surgeries, medical education and research into other blinding eye diseases such as glaucoma and diseases of the retina.

Say “YES” to Eye Donation and Sign-up to be a Donor.

Join the millions of New Yorkers who are signing up with the New York State Donate Life Registry to be a donor at the time of death. The registry is an electronic donor card.

SIGN UP

Most importantly: Say “Yes" to donation if asked about a family member who hasn’t signed up to be a donor. Give the beautiful Gift of Sight.

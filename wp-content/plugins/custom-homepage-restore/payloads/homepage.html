Resources

The Eye-Bank provides quality donor tissue for all cornea transplant procedures and ophthalmic research. We also offer resources and volunteer opportunities to transplant recipients and donor family members whose lives have been touched by the gifts of eye donors.

The world’s first eye bank

Since its founding in 1944, The Eye-Bank has helped restore sight to more than 76,000 individuals and has influenced eye banking practices worldwide.

Eye Can See Again

The Eye-Bank recently launched a new website to celebrate the gift of sight.

EyeCanSeeAgain.org features a library of transplant recipient testimonial videos and a portrait gallery of New Yorkers whose sight was restored with donor tissue provided by The Eye-Bank.

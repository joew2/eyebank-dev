# Homepage Resources photograph framing — November 1, 2026

1. Install **14-custom-resource-photo-framing-1.0.0.zip** as a new plugin.
2. Activate **Custom Resource Photo Framing CWS**.
3. Open **Tools → Resource Photo Framing CWS**.
4. Check the backup agreement and click **Process all updates**.
5. Clear the site/CDN cache and review the Resources card on desktop and mobile.

The Transplant Recipients & Donor Families photo is enlarged by 5%. Desktop requests a 30px shift right; mobile requests 30px right and 20px down. The offsets are capped where necessary to prevent empty edges and keep the man's head visible. The existing card/link and other Resources photos remain unchanged. No theme update is needed.

The adjustment is also applied locally. After confirming the live result, the plugin may be deactivated; its saved CSS remains.
